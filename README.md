# kcarlson4943.github.io
This will be a site where you can make a piece of art on 16 by 16 pixel  grid. You will also be able to make another piece of art "outside the box" if your is really good.
## Run instructions 
simply click the https://kcarlson4943.github.io/Pixel.github.io/ link and have fun making art.
The site will delete your art each time you click the "submit" button which can be annoying but its also fun to try multiple times.
Good luck making approved art and have fun!!
